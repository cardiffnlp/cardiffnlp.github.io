summary: Talk by Dr. Elena Kochkina (JP Morgan Chase)
abstract: "Financial claim verification requires models to reason over complex statements and assess whether they are supported by evidence. In this talk I would like to cover two research papers contributing to improving parts of the verification pipeline. I’ll begin with FinNLI, a benchmark for financial natural language inference built from SEC filings, annual reports, and earnings-call transcripts. I’ll then discuss Distill and Align Decomposition (DAD) method, which breaks complex statements into verifiable subclaims and aligns them with a downstream verifier. I’ll close with open challenges in claim verification and financial NLP and other research directions our lab is pursuing."

**Invited Speaker:** Dr. Elena Kochkina (JP Morgan Chase)

**Short Bio:**
Dr. Elena Kochkina is an AI Research Lead at JPMorgan Chase, with expertise in Tackling Online Misinformation using Deep Learning and, more broadly, automated methods against Online Harms. Enthusiastic about cutting-edge research with positive societal impact. Prior to joining JPMorgan Chase, she was a Postdoctoral Researcher at Queen Mary University of London and the Alan Turing Institute, where she investigated the use of Natural Language Processing to combat online misinformation, with a particular focus on COVID-19-related misinformation. Her research was funded by UKRI through the PANACEA project. She holds a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Warwick.
